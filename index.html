What is a sound bath?

A sound bath is a guided rest. You lie down comfortably while slow, layered tones from singing bowls fill the room. There is nothing to practise and nothing to get right.

Many people feel calmer, sleep a little easier, and leave with a clearer head. Beginners are very welcome.

What to expect

Sessions are unhurried and quiet. Bring a mat or blanket if you like; a few are available if you forget.
